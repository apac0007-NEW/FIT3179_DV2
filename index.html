Who makes community events happen?

Festivals get the headlines, but community life runs on volunteers and thousands of smaller gatherings. First, who gives their time across Australia. Then, what that looks like in one city.

Part 1: Who gives their time

Festivals, markets and clubs run on volunteers. Here is who volunteers across Australia.

Where Australians volunteer

About 22.6% of Australians aged 15 and over volunteered through an organisation in 2025. Tasmania leads at 27.5%, and Victoria is lowest at 21.8%.

Every state and territory fell since 2020, and the ACT fell most, from 30.9% to 22.0%.

Cities, towns and the bush

People in remote areas are the most likely to volunteer (34.0%), compared with 20.9% in major cities. Small communities lean on their neighbours.

Volunteering through organisations has fallen from 36.2% in 2010 to 22.6% in 2025.

Who volunteers, and for what

People aged 35 to 54 volunteer most, and women aged 35 to 44 lead at 30.5%.

Sport is the biggest cause at 33.3% of volunteers, followed by religious groups and community or cultural groups, which include many festivals.

What stops people

The top reason is no time: 47% of non-volunteers say so, rising to 57.7% for ages 25 to 44. Half of volunteers say they started because someone they knew was involved or asked them (50.3%).

Some small figures carry a high margin of error, so read them as a guide.

Part 2: What is on in one city

Brisbane City Council lists 1,998 events between 29 October and 19 November 2026. Here is where, when and for whom they happen.

Where events happen

About 33% of events are held in parks and 31% in libraries, so most of the city's social life happens in shared public space.

Look for the darker squares on the second map: events cluster around the CBD and a handful of suburban centres.

When they happen

The busiest day is Thursday 12 November with 121 events. Only 23% start on a weekend, so this is a weekday city.

The maps on the right split the same venues by weekday and weekend.

What kinds of events

Fitness & wellbeing is the biggest group at 49% of events. Only 6 listings are tagged as festivals, so big celebrations sit on top of a much larger base of everyday community activity.

Click a kind in the stream chart to follow it through the weeks.

What it takes to join

74% of events are free, so cost is rarely a barrier. Booking is a bigger hurdle for some kinds of event than others.
